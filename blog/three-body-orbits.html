Stable Orbits in the Three-Body Problem

Local first pass ·
N-body Leapfrog Simulation

Three-body motion is famous for being unstable in general, but not every orbit flies apart. Some initial conditions fall into beautiful repeating patterns. This first pass simulates Newtonian gravity live with a fast leapfrog stepper and uses trails to make the structure of each orbit visible.

What is periodic here?

These presets use central configurations or well-known periodic initial conditions. Energy drift is shown live so numerical error stays visible.

Why leapfrog?

Leapfrog uses one force evaluation per step and is a natural fit for orbital systems, so the simulation can stay live and fast.

What to try

Increase the time step until the figure-eight deforms, then reset and compare it with the Lagrange triangle.

Closed shapes

The triangle and line presets are central configurations: the whole shape rotates while the relative geometry stays fixed.
